\paragraph{Cohort composition.}
The three HuggingFace samples use non-overlapping addresses and known campaign
groups, which prevents their direct reuse between samples. Repeated exclusions
also change the population that remains. The 40-role controlled cohort
contains 503 malicious addresses and none of them is a contract account. Its
result cannot establish whether local motif-role features help detect malicious contracts.
Two samples use 32 motif roles, while the remaining sample uses all 40. The
analyses therefore test the same main question on different samples, but
they are not identical replications of one feature definition.
