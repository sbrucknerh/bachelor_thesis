This chapter presents the results for the four research questions. RQ1 and RQ2
are examined in three non-overlapping HuggingFace samples. Each contains a
randomly drawn prevalence cohort and a controlled cohort in which benign and
malicious addresses are matched by activity. The
two 32-role samples contain only star and two-node motif roles. The 40-role
sample also includes triangles. RQ3 and RQ4 use the nine
Real-CATS categories with enough active addresses for the analysis. Real-CATS
also provides a second binary comparison for RQ1 and RQ2. BLTE was prepared as
an optional dataset, but it is not part of the results reported here.

The chapter reports the measured scores, window selections, and statistical
comparisons. Their interpretation and possible explanations are discussed in
Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion}.
